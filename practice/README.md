# Practices

Practices are ways of engaging the SWARM loop in real work.

They are not instructions.

They are not step-by-step methods.

They are entry points into exploration.

---

## How to Use a Practice

A practice is something you enter, not something you execute.

You may:

- move through sections nonlinearly
- repeat parts of the practice multiple times
- stop and restart as signals emerge

There is no requirement to complete a practice in a single pass.

---

## What to Focus On

Practices are designed to:

- surface signals
- sharpen judgment
- reveal what matters

The goal is not to “finish” the practice.

The goal is to notice what emerges while using it.

---

## What Practices Are Not

Practices are not:

- checklists
- rigid workflows
- guaranteed outcomes

If a practice feels mechanical, you are likely outside the loop.

---

## Relationship to the Protocol

Practices sit on top of the SWARM protocol.

They provide a way to apply the loop in specific contexts.

Use the protocol to understand how SWARM works.

Use practices to apply it.

---

## Evolving Practices

Practices may change over time.

As new signals emerge, practices can be refined, expanded, or replaced.

They are part of the system, not separate from it.
