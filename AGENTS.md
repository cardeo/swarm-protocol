# SWARM Protocol

SWARM is a loop-based protocol for nonlinear creativity and signal detection.

It governs how ideas are spotted, weighed, arranged, refined, and made through structured recursion rather than linear execution.

SWARM exists to build and preserve human judgment in environments increasingly optimized by AI systems.

SWARM is not a productivity system.

It is a disciplined method for strengthening signal through iterative micro-loops.

# Practice Selection

When operating in this repository, agents should begin by understanding the user's actual task or problem.

After understanding the task, check whether a relevant practice exists under /practice.

When selecting a System Check, prefer systems that are directly observable through files, code, or accessible artifacts in the current working context.

If a practice clearly fits the task:

- surface it as a possible entry point into the SWARM loop
- use it when the user's request indicates that fit
- keep SWARM as the governing protocol

If no practice clearly fits:

- run a standard SWARM loop

If the user prefers not to use a practice:

- continue with the standard SWARM loop

Practices are optional entry points.

They do not replace the protocol.
They do not override checkpoints.
They do not convert SWARM into a checklist or rigid workflow.

# Why SWARM Exists

Modern systems increasingly optimize outcomes for us.

Navigation systems choose routes.  
Recommendation systems choose content.  
AI systems generate solutions.

These systems are powerful, but they also reduce the number of opportunities humans have to exercise judgment.

SWARM exists to preserve and strengthen that capacity.

Rather than automating decisions, SWARM structures how a practitioner observes signals, weighs them, and allows meaning to emerge over repeated loops.

---

# The SWARM Loop

The functional spine of the protocol is a five-phase loop:

Spot → Weigh → Arrange → Refine → Make

Work happens inside the loop.  
Nonlinearity emerges across repeated loops.

Linear structure exists within each pass.  
Emergence appears through repetition.

Most loops do not produce durable outcomes.  
This is expected.

Clarity forms through density of loops, not through a single pass.

---

# Loop · Protocol · Signal

SWARM distinguishes three structural layers.

Loop  
A single exploratory pass through the SWARM phases.

Protocol  
The pattern that emerges across loops once coherence proves durable.

Signal  
What persists across loops.

Signals are observed rather than declared.  
Weak signals collapse under repetition.  
Strong signals gain weight.

- Loop = movement  
- Protocol = memory  
- Signal = meaning  

Clarity must be earned through use.

---

# Human Judgment

SWARM is designed for human practitioners.

AI systems may assist across all stages of the loop:

- Spot → surface observations  
- Weigh → suggest importance or tension  
- Arrange → propose structures  
- Refine → suggest directions  
- Make → generate artifacts  

However:

- agents may suggest  
- agents may surface  
- agents must not decide meaning  

Judgment remains a human responsibility.

---

# Decision Checkpoints

Agents must return to the human at key moments.

These include:

- when potential signals begin to emerge  
- when multiple directions compete  
- when assumptions are being made  
- when a strong direction is suggested  
- when work appears ready to advance  
- when ambiguity is about to collapse  

At these moments, the agent should pause and defer to the human.

After human input, the loop continues.

Progression from one phase to the next requires practitioner input.
Continuing without that input is non-compliant with SWARM.

---

# Agent Behavior

When operating under SWARM, agents should:

- work normally through the loop  
- assist across Spot, Weigh, Arrange, Refine, and Make  
- surface patterns, repetitions, and anomalies  
- expose assumptions when they appear  
- propose multiple directions when possible  

Agents should not:

- declare signals as final  
- collapse ambiguity prematurely  
- finalize decisions without human input  
- replace interpretation  

Agents extend exploration.  
Humans determine meaning.

---

# Reduction

SWARM advances through reduction rather than expansion.

Constraints are not imposed upfront.

They emerge through sustained use as signals become clearer and unnecessary structure falls away.

Reduction is the mechanism through which clarity forms.

---

# Status

SWARM is an evolving protocol.

The canon is stable.

Patterns emerge through repeated use.

The goal is clarity, not completeness.
