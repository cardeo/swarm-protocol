# SWARM Protocol — Development Notes

## Purpose

SWARM Protocol is the public, stable articulation of the SWARM thinking system.

The repository exists so that humans and AI agents can reference the protocol and integrate it into their workflows.

Goal:

Clone SWARM → feed it to local agents → integrate into workflows.

The protocol is designed to be portable, human-readable, and agent-readable.

---

## Launch Threshold

SWARM Protocol can launch publicly once the following exist:

• Stable canon documents  
• 5–10 applied patterns  
• README explaining the protocol  
• AGENTS.md defining agent constraints

Playbooks are not required for launch.

Patterns should be small and modular.

---

## Repository Philosophy

The repository is intentionally minimal.

Documents are written in plain Markdown.

The goal is long-term portability rather than tooling dependency.

The protocol should remain understandable even if GitHub disappears.

---

## Canon vs Patterns

Canon explains the philosophy of SWARM.

Patterns explain how SWARM is practiced.

Canon should remain small and stable.

Patterns will expand gradually as applications of the protocol become clearer.

---

## Role of AI Systems

SWARM is designed for human practitioners.

AI systems may assist the loop but must not replace human judgment.

Agents may help with:

• retrieving patterns  
• summarizing observations  
• organizing signals  
• prompting reflection

Agents must not:

• collapse loops prematurely  
• declare signals  
• automate judgment

---

## Relationship to Loop Lab

Loop Lab is the research environment for SWARM.

New ideas and experiments happen there.

Concepts may graduate into SWARM Protocol once they become stable and repeatable.

Loop Lab → discovery  
SWARM Protocol → stable knowledge

---

## Structural Layers

The broader ecosystem currently includes:

Loop Lab → research and experimentation  
SWARM Protocol → stable thinking protocol  
Cardeo Vault → personal knowledge archive

These layers should remain separate to prevent conceptual drift.

---

## Future Expansion

Possible future additions:

• Playbooks (collections of patterns)  
• Practitioner guides  
• SWARM companion tools  
• Training materials

The protocol itself should remain lightweight.

Expansion should occur primarily through patterns.

---

## Drift Risks

Risks to monitor as the protocol evolves:

• SWARM becoming metaphor instead of operational protocol  
• Patterns becoming checklists instead of loops  
• Over-automation by AI tools  
• Losing the Weigh phase  
• Mistaking trends for signals

These risks mirror those documented in the canon.

## Distribution Strategy

SWARM Protocol should be openly accessible.

The goal is for practitioners and AI systems to be able to clone the protocol and integrate it into their workflows.

Adoption and practice are prioritized over early monetization.

Monetization may emerge later through services, tools, and applied systems.

## Origin

The SWARM Protocol repository emerged during work on the Cardeo Vault
architecture after a discussion with Kris about local AI systems and
agent-readable knowledge structures.

The protocol is designed to be cloneable so practitioners can integrate
SWARM into their own local AI workflows.
